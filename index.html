🚋 The Muni Challenge

Ride every Muni line in one day — ending at Bus Stop, Union Street.

Rules

  • Ride at least 3 blocks on every line.
  • No transfer walk over 5 blocks.
  • Express/rapid variants count as their base line (1X, 5R, 8AX/8BX, 9R, 14R, 28R, 30X, 38R).
  • Finish at Bus Stop, Union St.

Best day: Tuesday–Thursday. Avoids Monday holiday schedules and Friday evening crowds, and every line runs at full weekday frequency.
